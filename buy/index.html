Configure your wall

Choose the wall colour and mounting, then pick a pot and click a hole. A pot blocks the holes around it. Click a dashed tile to add a wall above, below, left or right. Neighbouring walls are joined by two dovetail fillers per shared edge.

Your order

Order ID (position and ID of each wall)

Wall ID has three parts separated by dots. 1) mounting letter (D = DIN rail, N = nail holes, G = glue) and colour number. 2) 9 pot holes in reading order (A = PotSmall, B = PotLarge, - = empty). 3) 8 dovetail holes, two per side clockwise from the top (F = filler, - = no neighbouring wall). Example: D1.A---B----.--FF----. R1C2 means row 1, column 2 of the layout. The assembly robot reads the ID and picks the matching premade set.

GreenGrid is a student project at NTNU (MMA4002). It is a prototype: no real orders are taken, copy the order ID to keep your configuration.
