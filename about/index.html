About us

Three students, one green wall

GreenGrid started in the course MMA4002 Design for Automated Manufacturing at NTNU. The question: how can a product be customised by the customer and still be built simply and repeatably by a robot?

How we make it

  • Everything is 3D printed in PLA, so the interfaces are designed around printing tolerances.
  • Two printing flows: walls take the longest, so they are printed ahead and kept in stock. Pots and fillers are printed only when you order.
  • One ID per wall describes mounting, colour, pots and joints. A collaborative robot (Omron TM5) reads it and assembles the wall.
  • Modular by design: every choice you make maps to one part, so new pot shapes need no new wall.
